## Data Protection and Compliance — Compliance and Data Governance

### Regulatory framework
Thames Valley University is a UK institution. UK GDPR applies to the processing of student health data. HealthCore will act as a data processor. We will process health data only for the referral network program. Health data is special category data under Article 9 of UK GDPR. We will rely on explicit consent or substantial public interest as the lawful basis. We will provide only aggregate, de-identified program reports to the university. No individual health records will be shared with the client.

### Data Processing Agreement (DPA) under UK GDPR
We will include a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) compliant with UK GDPR Article 28. The DPA will cover data subject rights, breach notification, and data minimization. It will specify the lawful basis for processing health data. It will also include a 72-hour breach notification to the ICO. The DPA is mandatory for this engagement.

### Compliance approval
Compliance approval is mandatory before signing. Claire Whitfield, Chief Compliance Officer, must approve the final proposal and DPA. This step is not optional and cannot be waived.
